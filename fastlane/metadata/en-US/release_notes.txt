Thank you for using RunRun!

【New】
・Block users: use the "…" menu on a profile to block someone. Blocked users no longer appear in rankings, search, or friend requests. Manage them in Settings → Blocked Users
・Report users: report inappropriate display names or abusive behavior from the "…" menu on a profile

【Improved】
・Display names are now limited to 20 characters with no line breaks or leading/trailing spaces

【Fixed】
・Fixed an issue where notifications such as friend requests could stop arriving after switching devices or reinstalling the app
